Features

Everything you need to see the real workday

Built for agencies and remote teams who bill by the hour and want the receipts — without chasing anyone for a timesheet.

📸

Screenshots every 10 minutes

A low-resolution, timestamped capture of the display you're actually working on. Light on bandwidth, stored safely in the cloud.

⚡️

Activity levels

Keyboard and mouse activity per minute, tracked every minute and shown next to every screenshot — so idle time is obvious.

🪟

Apps & websites

See which app or site was in front for each tracked minute. No guessing where the afternoon went.

🔔

Idle warnings

When activity hits zero, the app asks: keep working or clock out? The timer pauses until you answer, and admins get the alert.

👥

Team dashboard

Admins see everyone — live status, hours, activity and the latest screenshot — without switching accounts.

📊

Analytics & timesheets

Daily and weekly trends, activity rings, top apps and per-person timesheets. Export-ready numbers for invoicing.

How it works

Three steps, about two minutes

Each employee installs the app once and signs in. Everything after that is automatic.

1

Download & install

Pick your platform below and install the desktop app. It lives in your menu bar and stays out of the way.

2

Sign in and clock in

Create an account with your work email, verify the code we send you, and hit Clock in when the day begins.

3

Watch it fill in

Screenshots, activity and app usage sync to the web automatically. Admins open the dashboard from any browser.

Employees

Track with the desktop app

Time is tracked only in the app — that's where you clock in and out. Your own activity and hours are always visible to you there.

  • Clock in / clock out
  • Activity every minute, a screenshot every 10
  • Idle warnings that pause the timer
Admins

Monitor from the web

The web dashboard is monitoring only: no clocking in here. Admins see every member's day, screenshots, analytics and warnings.

  • Live team dashboard
  • Screenshot timeline per person
  • Analytics, timesheets & warnings
